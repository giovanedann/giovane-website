## Introduction

If you read [Why I Love Serverless](/blog/why-i-love-serverless) you know 2 things: I love serverless architectures, and the basics of how they work.

I am a huge fan of AWS Lambda, and I love working with it, but, there's a lot of things that can bring a bad experience if you aren't careful: bundle size, cold-starts, isolated containers (memory sharing), local development (that's a real pain) and everything else.

This year, while researching best technologies for building a personal project, I found out about Cloudflare Workers, and I don't think I'll ever go back to Lambdas for building serverless APIs again.

In this post, I'll break it down for you to understand the whys about this decision, and deep dive about why Cloudflare Workers are so cool.
